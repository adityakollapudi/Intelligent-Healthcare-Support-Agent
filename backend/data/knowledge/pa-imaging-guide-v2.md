# Synthetic Imaging Prior Authorization Guide (Version 2.0)

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Fictional prior authorization documentation requirements for the IHSA prototype. A complete document packet is not an approval.

## Lumbar spine MRI (SYN-PROC-MRI-LUMBAR)
A prior authorization request for a lumbar spine MRI must include:
- The requested service date.
- The ordering provider's 10-digit identifier.
- Documentation of the duration of conservative therapy. The synthetic guideline expects at least six weeks of conservative therapy such as physical therapy or medication.
- The member number in the format SYN-M-000000.
- A referral letter from the referring provider.
- A supporting rationale, which an authorized staff member reviews manually.

## Submission timeline
Requests should be submitted at least 7 business days before the requested service date. Urgent requests can be marked as expedited.

## What happens after submission
Submitted packets are checked for completeness and then reviewed by an authorized reviewer. Documentation completeness does not guarantee approval or coverage. Decisions are communicated to the ordering provider.
