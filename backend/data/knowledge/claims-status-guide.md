# Synthetic Claims Status Guide

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Explains the claim statuses used in the IHSA prototype. It does not contain any member's claim data.

## What each claim status means
- **Received**: the claim has arrived and is waiting to be processed. Most claims move to Under processing within 3 business days.
- **Under processing**: the claim is being reviewed against the plan's benefits. Processing normally finishes within 30 calendar days of receipt.
- **Pending information**: processing is paused because more information is needed. The claim stays pending until the requested information arrives or 45 days pass, whichever comes first.
- **Processed – paid**: processing is complete and payment was issued to the provider or the member.
- **Processed – denied**: processing is complete and the claim was not paid. The denial reason is listed on the explanation of benefits, and the member may appeal.

## Common reason codes
- **PI-01**: an itemized bill is requested from the provider.
- **PI-02**: referral documentation is requested.
- **DN-04**: the service is not listed in the plan's benefits.
- **PD-00**: processed and paid with no adjustments.

## What to do when a claim is pending information
If a claim is pending information, the member or provider should send the requested document within 45 days. Documents can be sent through the provider portal or attached to a support case. When the document is received, processing resumes automatically.

## Checking a claim status
Members can see the current status of their own claims on the My claims screen. The IHSA assistant explains what statuses mean but does not change claim statuses.
