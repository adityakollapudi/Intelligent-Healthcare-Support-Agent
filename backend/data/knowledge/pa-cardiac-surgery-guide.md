# Synthetic Cardiac Surgery Prior Authorization Guide

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Fictional requirements for the IHSA prototype. Not clinical guidance.

## Heart bypass surgery (SYN-PROC-CARDIAC-CABG)
Prior authorization is needed for coronary artery bypass graft surgery, also called heart bypass surgery. The request must include:
- The member number in the format SYN-M-000000.
- The requested service date.
- The ordering provider's 10-digit identifier.
- Documentation of the duration of medical therapy tried before surgery.
- A referral letter from the referring cardiologist.
- A clinical note summary from the treating provider.
- A supporting rationale, which an authorized staff member reviews manually.

## What readiness means
A complete request means the documentation is ready for human review. It is not an approval, and it does not confirm that the surgery is covered by a plan.
