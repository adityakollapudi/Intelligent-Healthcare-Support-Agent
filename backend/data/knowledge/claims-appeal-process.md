# Synthetic Claim Appeal Process

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. A fictional administrative process for the IHSA prototype.

## Who can appeal
A member, or a provider acting with the member's written consent, can appeal a claim with the status Processed – denied.

## Appeal deadline
An appeal must be filed within 180 calendar days of the date on the explanation of benefits.

## How to file an appeal
1. Complete the Synthetic Appeal Request Form.
2. Include the claim number, the reason for the appeal and any supporting documents, such as an itemized bill or a letter from the treating provider.
3. Submit the form through the member portal or attach it to a support case.

## Appeal decision timeline
A standard appeal is decided within 30 calendar days of receipt. An expedited appeal, available when a delay could seriously affect the member's health, is decided within 72 hours. The IHSA assistant cannot decide appeals; decisions are made by authorized reviewers.
