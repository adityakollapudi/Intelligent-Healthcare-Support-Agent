# General Member FAQ (Synthetic)

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Fictional content for the IHSA internship prototype. It does not describe any real insurer, plan or Evernorth policy.

## Updating contact details
Members can update their mailing address, phone number and email in the member portal under Profile > Contact details. Changes made in the portal take effect within 2 business days. Address changes requested by phone take up to 5 business days.

## Member ID cards
A digital member ID card is available in the member portal immediately after enrollment. A printed card is mailed within 10 business days of enrollment. Members can request a replacement printed card at no cost up to 3 times per plan year.

## Choosing a primary care provider
Members of the Synthetic Gold Plan and the Synthetic Bronze Plan are encouraged, but not required, to choose a primary care provider (PCP). A PCP can be selected or changed in the member portal; the change applies from the first day of the next month.

## Support hours
The synthetic member support line operates Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time. The member portal and the IHSA assistant are available at any time. Support requests that need a person are handled through support cases.

## Language assistance
Interpreter services are available at no cost when contacting member support. Members can ask for an interpreter at the start of the call.
