# Synthetic Gold Plan Benefit Guide — Plan Year 2026

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Plan code SYN-PLAN-GOLD-01. Fictional benefits for the IHSA prototype only; not a real coverage document.

## Primary care and specialists
An in-network primary care office visit has a $20 copay. An in-network specialist office visit has a $40 copay. Out-of-network office visits are subject to 40% coinsurance after the deductible.

## Preventive care
One annual preventive visit is covered at $0 in network. Preventive visits are not covered out of network.

## Imaging
In-network outpatient MRI is subject to 20% coinsurance. Outpatient MRI requires prior authorization before the service date; see the Synthetic Imaging Prior Authorization Guide. In-network X-rays have a $0 copay.

## Physical therapy
Outpatient physical therapy has a $25 copay per in-network visit. Coverage is limited to 20 visits per plan year. Visits beyond the 20-visit limit require prior authorization.

## Emergency care
An emergency room visit has a $150 copay, in or out of network. The copay is waived if the member is admitted to the hospital.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum
The individual in-network deductible is $750 per plan year. The individual in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $4,000 per plan year.
