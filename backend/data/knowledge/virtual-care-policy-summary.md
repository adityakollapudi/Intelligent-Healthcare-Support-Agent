# Synthetic Virtual Care Policy Summary

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Deliberately conflicts with the Synthetic Virtual Care FAQ for testing.

## Virtual visit cost sharing
Virtual primary care visits through the synthetic telehealth service have a $15 copay for members of the Synthetic Gold Plan and a $30 copay for members of the Synthetic Bronze Plan.

## Eligible services
Virtual visits cover routine primary care questions, prescription renewals and follow-up visits. They do not cover emergencies; in an emergency, members should call emergency services.
