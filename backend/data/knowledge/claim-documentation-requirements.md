# Synthetic Claim Documentation Requirements

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Fictional administrative requirements for the IHSA prototype.

## Member reimbursement claims
When a member paid a provider directly and asks for reimbursement, the claim must include:
- A completed Synthetic Member Reimbursement Form.
- An itemized bill showing each service, its date and its cost.
- Proof of payment, such as a receipt.
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 365 days of the service date.

## Out-of-network claims
Out-of-network providers may not submit claims on the member's behalf. In that case the member submits a reimbursement claim with the documents listed above.

## Accepted document formats
Documents can be uploaded as PDF, PNG or JPEG files up to 10 MB each. Photos of receipts must be readable.
