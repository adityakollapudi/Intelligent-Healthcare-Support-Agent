# Synthetic Imaging Prior Authorization Guide (Version 1.0)

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. This version has been replaced by version 2.0 and is kept only for traceability.

## Lumbar spine MRI (SYN-PROC-MRI-LUMBAR)
A prior authorization request for a lumbar spine MRI must include:
- The requested service date.
- The ordering provider's identifier.
- Documentation of at least four weeks of conservative therapy.
- The member number.

## Submission timeline
Requests should be submitted at least 5 business days before the service date.
