# Synthetic Support Agent Procedures (Internal)

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Internal audience: support agents. Not shown to members or providers.

## Queue handling
Agents take unassigned cases from the queue oldest first, except cases with the escalation reason Approval required, which are taken first.

## Escalating to a supervisor
An agent escalates a case to a supervisor when a member reports a possible privacy incident, when a case has been open for more than 10 business days, or when two agents disagree on a resolution.

## Notes
Case notes must not include full document contents. Refer to documents by their file name.
