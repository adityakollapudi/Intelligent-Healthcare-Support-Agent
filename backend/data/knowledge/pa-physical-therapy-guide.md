# Synthetic Physical Therapy Prior Authorization Guide

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Fictional requirements for the IHSA prototype.

## Extended physical therapy (SYN-PROC-PT-EXTENDED)
Prior authorization is needed for outpatient physical therapy visits beyond the plan's annual visit limit. The request must include:
- The member number.
- A referral dated within 30 days before the request date.
- A clinical note summary describing progress so far.
- The visit history for the current plan year, which an authorized staff member reviews manually.

## Number of additional visits
Each approved extension, if granted by a reviewer, covers up to 10 additional visits. A new request is needed for further visits.
