# Synthetic Bronze Plan Benefit Guide — Plan Year 2026

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Plan code SYN-PLAN-BRONZE-01. Fictional benefits for the IHSA prototype only; not a real coverage document.

## Primary care and specialists
An in-network primary care office visit has a $45 copay. An in-network specialist office visit has an $80 copay. Out-of-network office visits are subject to 50% coinsurance after the deductible.

## Preventive care
One annual preventive visit is covered at $0 in network.

## Imaging
In-network outpatient MRI is subject to 40% coinsurance and requires prior authorization. Out-of-network MRI is not covered.

## Physical therapy
Outpatient physical therapy has a $50 copay per in-network visit and is limited to 12 visits per plan year. Out-of-network physical therapy is not covered.

## Emergency care
An emergency room visit has a $350 copay, in or out of network.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum
The individual in-network deductible is $3,000 per plan year. The individual in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $8,500 per plan year.
